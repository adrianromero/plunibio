## Start here

If this is your first time using Plunibio, begin with [Getting started]({{ '/en/docs/getting-started/' | relative_url }}). You will learn how a workspace is organized, how to open a file, and how to save your first change.

You can also go directly to a specific task from the contents below. The manual describes Plunibio's behavior and calls out differences between Windows, Linux, and macOS whenever they affect a procedure.
